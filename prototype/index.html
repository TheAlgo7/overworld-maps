Android Auto, simulated. The dark rail, the ETA box and the round buttons are drawn by Android Auto itself from the app's turn data, and the app may only tint the turn card. The map underneath is ours, using a calmer car cut of the same theme: wider roads, no texture or glow, fewer labels.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, tiles from OpenFreeMap (OpenMapTiles schema). Route recorded once from the OSRM demo server: Chhatarpur to India Gate.

The three themes are original work inspired by open-world game maps. No game artwork, icons, fonts or names are used.
